About OmniCalc

A private, offline-first calculator platform for Windows and Android.

About OmniCalc

OmniCalc is a comprehensive mathematics application that brings many calculators and tools into one place: standard and scientific calculators, graphing, an equation solver, matrices, vectors, complex numbers, statistics, number theory, number bases, unit and custom conversions, percentages, date and time tools, and a library of formulas.

Purpose

Everyday maths rarely stays in one category. A bill needs a percentage, a homework problem needs a quadratic solved and graphed, a recipe needs a unit conversion. Keeping these tools together means one app to learn, one history, one set of favorites and one look.

Philosophy

  • Usability. A home screen of clearly named tools, search, and keyboard shortcuts on Windows.
  • Accessibility. Adjustable text size, a high-contrast option, reduced motion and light, dark or system themes. Accessibility has been checked by automated tests; a full screen-reader review has not been done yet.
  • Practical functionality. Results are checked where possible (equation roots are substituted back) and labelled honestly as exact, approximate or partial.
  • Simplicity and privacy. No account, no ads, no analytics.

Technologies

OmniCalc is built with React and TypeScript, bundled with Vite, packaged for Windows with Electron and for Android with Capacitor. A shared math core powers every version. It uses math.js for expression parsing, Luxon for dates and time zones and Plotly for graphs, and is tested with Vitest.
