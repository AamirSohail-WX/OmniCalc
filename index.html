    Why OmniCalc

    Built to stay out of your way

    • Many tools, one app. Switch between calculators without installing something new for each job.
    • Private and offline-first. No account, no ads and no analytics. Calculations and saved data stay on your device. The only network feature is an optional, user-started download of exchange rates.
    • Windows and Android. Both versions share the same math core.
    • Comfortable to read. Light, dark or system theme, adjustable text size and a high-contrast option.
    • Honest results. Exact fractions are labelled exact, estimates are labelled estimates, and incomplete factorizations are labelled partial.
